# Rex — launch video script (draft 1)

Kinetic launch style (launch-video skill), 16:9, target 52–56 s at ~2.6 spoken words/s.
Markup: `[pill]` = Rex Red pill on the key word · `*accent*` = red words · `_serif_` = italic aside.
Every claim is something Rex does today (tested this week); no invented users or numbers.

the cut lands at about 55 s.

## Hook options (pick one; all true)
- A (above): "You asked for one thing. / 'Send Shanky the cheapest flights to Bhopal.' / Your assistant sent back… a paragraph."
- B: "Your assistant can talk. / Can it _do_ anything?"
- C: "Twelve tabs. Three apps. One message to Shanky."

## Open items before building
- CTA link: GitHub repo, a demo URL, or the hackathon page?
- Voice: your own recording, a Fish Audio voice, or an ElevenLabs voice (the free macOS voice is used for timing either way).
- Format: 16:9 (X, LinkedIn, YouTube) or 9:16 (Reels, Shorts).
- Openverse backgrounds: only images whose licence allows commercial use (CC0 / CC BY, with credits in the post).
