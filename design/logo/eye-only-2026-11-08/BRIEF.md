# Rex — eye-only logo exploration

2026-11-08 · Johny / frontend identity · Concept checkpoint, not an approved identity.

Rex is a multilingual AI voice operator. Assumptions: playful, capable, attentive and approachable; exact name Rex; preserve the current two-eye mascot and red #FF4055. Intended uses: dashboard header, app icon, favicon and social avatar. No tagline. No dashboard code changes in this exploration.

## Strategy
Word map: Rex → character, presence, confidence; voice → reply, rhythm, turn; operator → ready, action, focus. Intersection: a small character that is ready to act. Avoid category clichés: AI sparkles, knots, brains, microphone silhouettes, chat bubbles and generic blue gradients. Local skill library study covered AI/ML conventions and negative-space construction in Framer, Pagekit, Patreon and Kotlin; studied as construction examples, never traced.

## Construction and refinement
All marks are filled geometric paths with true transparent counters, no fonts, rasters, gradients or masks. Symbols use a 256-unit square. Presence uses a 224-unit capsule, semicircular ends, and ±15° eye pills. Ready uses a 48-unit stem, circular bowl, capsule counter and 45° leg. Reply reuses the existing custom Rex lettering as brand equity; no font dependency.

v1: inspected large white-background renders and audited paths. R leg overflowed the artboard; repaired in v2. Presence eyes were too narrow at icon sizes. Reply horizontal e was legible but less characteristic.
v2: enlarged eye apertures, increased capsule height, shortened R leg, restored the custom 15° e crossbar. Inspected the final overview at actual 64/32/16 px and browser mono/reversed/background tests; the generated HTML also includes blur/mirror contexts. The standalone wordmark should be used above 64 px wide; Presence is the strongest small icon.

Recommendation: A / Presence. It keeps recognition between the logo and animated assistant. Its honest limitation is that two-eye capsule mascots are a familiar category; the custom wordmark should accompany it where space permits. B is more formal and less playful; C is distinctive lettering but weak as a favicon.

Trademark and reverse-image clearance have not been performed. Previous logo kit remains unchanged. Full kit and application integration follow the user's chosen direction.
