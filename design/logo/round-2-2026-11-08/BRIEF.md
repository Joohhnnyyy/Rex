# Rex — round two

User rejected all first-round concepts. This round moves away from the capsule face, generic R and old lowercase lettering. Keep red #FF4055 as the eventual colour; explore character, abstract and wordmark routes. No application integration or final kit before selection.

## Longlist and scores
Scores out of 25 combine clarity, distinction, simplicity, relevance and small-size strength; subjective design judgments.

1. Rexlet: a compact dinosaur head with an open jaw and one eye — pictorial, 22.
2. Duet: two open half-turns, one for listening and one for action — abstract, 21.
3. Stride: tall custom REX with a forward-cut R and a strong X — wordmark, 21.
4. Scout: a fox head made from an R counter — pictorial, 16.
5. Orbit R: a continuous orbital initial — letterform, 16; familiar tech territory.
6. Ping: a dot entering a curved return — abstract, 18.
7. Crown: a three-step lowercase r — letterform, 17; overly literal Rex/king cue.
8. Switch: two displaced blocks forming an X counter — abstract, 17.
9. Rex signature: single-line cursive name — wordmark, 16; weaker favicon.
10. Companion: a rounded diamond with one winking aperture — mascot, 15; too close to rejected face.

Chosen concepts span three mark types. Avoid AI knots, brains, microphones, sparkles, closed infinity loops and the previous capsule silhouette. Local library construction studies: Deno (silhouette and eye), Medium (primary geometric forms), Tailwind (paired curves). These are study references only; no traced paths. Dinosaur mascots and paired abstract curves are established categories, so uniqueness is not asserted.

## Geometry and tests
Rexlet: straight neck, circular forehead and snout radii, open jaw and true transparent circular eye. Duet: two open quarter-round turns, 48-unit band, generous central gap. Stride: hand-built filled uppercase letter paths; no font licence dependency.

v1 inspected in rendered overview; v2 enlarges the eye, opens the Duet gap and refines the R leg and E/X to clean 60° diagonals. Audited both rounds, inspected v2 actual 64/32/16-pixel ladder. Wordmark requires more width than icon concepts. Presentation is monochrome first; red #FF4055 is retained for the selected direction, not a finished variant kit.

Recommendation: Rexlet for a playful memorable character, Stride if the desired direction is more mature. Duet is the least literal but needs the name alongside it initially. Await user preference. Trademark clearance and reverse-image search not performed. Old concepts and dashboard unchanged.
